7 Fun Tricks to Teach Your Dog

Tricks build focus, confidence and your bond, and they're just as good for your dog's brain as "serious" training.

2 min read · Updated November 2026

Trick training is a great way to practice the skills behind all training: timing, small steps and rewards. Keep sessions short and fun. A clicker or marker word helps.

1. Hand target (touch)

Hold your flat hand near your dog's nose. When they sniff or bump it, mark and reward. Move your hand around. Useful for moving your dog without pulling, and for redirecting attention on walks.

2. Spin

Lure your dog in a circle with a treat at their nose. Mark and reward as they complete the circle. Fade the lure to a hand circle, then add "spin." Teach both directions.

3. Shake / paw

Hold a treat in a closed fist low by your dog's chest. When they paw at it, mark and reward. Then offer your open hand and reward when their paw touches it.

4. Bow

From standing, lure a treat down between your dog's front paws and slightly back. Mark the moment their elbows go down while their rear stays up. Reward quickly before they lie down.

5. Roll over

From a down, lure your dog's nose back over their shoulder so they roll onto their side, then continue the lure over. Do it in steps, rewarding each one. Use a soft surface.

6. Find it

Show your dog a treat, say "find it" and toss it a little away. Progress to hiding treats around a room. Great mental exercise.

7. Go to bed (place)

Toss a treat on your dog's bed. Mark and reward when they step on it, then when they lie down. Add "bed." Useful every day.

Tips

  • Break tricks into tiny steps. Reward progress, not perfection.
  • Stop while it's fun.
  • Avoid tricks that strain joints for puppies, seniors or dogs with back problems, such as lots of jumping or begging on hind legs.
