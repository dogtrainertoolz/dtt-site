The golden rules

Five habits behind every well-trained dog

Whatever you're teaching, these make it work faster.

Why reward-based training works
  1. Reward the behavior you want, the moment it happens.
  2. Keep sessions short: 3 to 5 minutes, a few times a day.
  3. Make it easy first, then add distance, duration and distractions.
  4. Manage the environment so mistakes can't become habits.
  5. Meet your dog's needs: exercise, sniffing, chewing and rest.

How we train

Reward-based
