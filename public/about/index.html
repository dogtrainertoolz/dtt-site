About Dog Trainer Toolz

Most dog owners don't need a dog trainer for every problem. They need a clear plan, broken into steps small enough to succeed at, and a few tools that take the guesswork out of it.

That's what Dog Trainer Toolz is for: free tools like a potty schedule generator and a treat calorie calculator, and step-by-step training plans for puppies, basic commands, leash walking and common problem behaviors.

How we train

Every plan on this site uses reward-based methods: treats, toys, play and praise. That's the approach supported by research and recommended by veterinary behavior organizations, and it's the one that keeps training fun for both of you. We don't recommend shock, prong or choke collars.

We're also honest about limits. Aggression, serious fear and separation panic need a veterinarian and a qualified behavior professional, and our guides say so.
