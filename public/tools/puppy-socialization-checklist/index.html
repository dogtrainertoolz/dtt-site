How to use it

Puppies have a sensitive period for socialization, roughly from 3 to 14 weeks of age, when new experiences are easiest to accept. That window overlaps with the time before vaccines are complete, so the goal is safe, positive exposure: carry your puppy, avoid areas where unknown dogs gather, and choose puppy classes that require vaccinations.

  • Quality beats quantity. Your puppy should stay relaxed. Pair each new thing with treats, keep it brief and let your puppy watch from a distance first.
  • Never force it. If your puppy hides, freezes or tries to escape, move further away and try again another day.
  • Keep going after 14 weeks. Socialization continues through adolescence.
