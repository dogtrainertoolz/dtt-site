## How it works The calculator uses the standard veterinary starting formula: **resting energy requirement (RER) = 70 × body weight in kg^0.75**, multiplied by a factor for life stage and activity. Most vets recommend keeping treats to **no more than 10% of daily calories**, so the rest of the diet stays balanced. These are estimates. Your dog's real needs depend on breed, metabolism and lifestyle, so check their body shape and weight every few weeks and adjust. Ask your vet for a target if your dog needs to lose weight. **Tip:** check the calories on your treat bag. Many soft training treats are 2–4 kcal each, but some biscuits and chews are 30 kcal or more.
