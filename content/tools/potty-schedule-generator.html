## How the schedule works A common rule of thumb is that puppies can hold their bladder for about **one hour per month of age, plus one**, up to around 8 hours, when they're awake and calm. Excitement, play, eating, drinking and waking up all make them need to go sooner. That's why the schedule adds breaks after meals and first thing in the morning. - **Under 12 weeks:** take your puppy out roughly every hour while awake, plus after every meal, nap and play session. - **Night trips:** young puppies usually need one or two trips during the night. Keep them quick, quiet and boring. - **Every puppy is different.** Use this as a starting point and adjust to your puppy's patterns.
