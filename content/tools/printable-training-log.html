## Why keep a log? Progress in dog training is often slow enough that you don't notice it. A simple log shows you what's working, when your dog is ready to move up a level, and when to make things easier. The 8-out-of-10 rule is a good guide: when your dog gets it right 8 times out of 10, add a little more distance, duration or distraction.
