1. **Repeating cues.** "Sit... sit... SIT!" teaches your dog that the first cue doesn't count. **Fix:** say it once. If your dog doesn't respond, make it easier and try again.

2. **Rewarding too late.** By the time the treat arrives, your dog has stood up. **Fix:** use a [marker word or clicker](/clicker-training-your-dog/) at the exact moment.

3. **Moving too fast.** Practicing in the kitchen, then expecting the same at a busy park. **Fix:** add distance, duration and distraction one at a time.

4. **Sessions that are too long.** **Fix:** 3–5 minutes, a few times a day. End on a success.

5. **Inconsistent rules.** One person allows sofa time, another doesn't. **Fix:** agree on rules and cue words as a household.

6. **Calling your dog for bad things.** Using "come" for baths and leaving the park. **Fix:** go get your dog for unpleasant things. See [recall](/how-to-teach-your-dog-recall/).

7. **Punishing after the fact.** Scolding a dog for an accident or chewed shoe they did an hour ago. **Fix:** dogs can't connect it. Manage better next time and clean up calmly.

8. **Rewards that aren't rewarding.** Dry biscuits can't compete with squirrels. **Fix:** use [higher-value treats](/best-dog-training-treats-for-better-results/) outdoors.

9. **Ignoring good behavior.** We tend to notice dogs only when they're naughty. **Fix:** catch your dog being calm and good, and reward it.

10. **Not meeting the dog's needs.** Under-exercised, bored or overtired dogs struggle to learn. **Fix:** daily exercise, sniffing, chewing and plenty of sleep. See [tiring out your dog](/how-to-tire-out-your-dog-easily/).
