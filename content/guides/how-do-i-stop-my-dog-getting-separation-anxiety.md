Some dogs become genuinely distressed when left alone. They're not getting revenge or being naughty; they're panicking. The good news is that it's treatable with patience.

## Signs

Usually only when alone or separated from their person:

- Barking, howling or whining
- Destruction around doors and windows
- Accidents in a house-trained dog
- Pacing, drooling, panting
- Escape attempts, sometimes with injuries

**Film your dog** when you leave. It's the best way to know what's really happening.

## Prevention for puppies and new dogs

- Practice **short alone times** from day one: in a pen or crate with a chew, while you're home but in another room.
- Make departures and arrivals **low-key**.
- Avoid having your new dog with you 24/7 for the first weeks, then suddenly leaving them for a full day.

## The plan for anxious dogs

1. **Stop leaving your dog alone beyond what they can cope with,** as much as possible during training. Use a dog sitter, daycare, a friend or bring your dog with you. Every panic episode sets progress back.
2. **Find your dog's starting point.** How long before signs of stress? 5 minutes? 30 seconds? Start below that.
3. **Practice tiny absences:** step out the door and come straight back. Build up very gradually, in seconds at first, watching for stress.
4. **Desensitize departure cues.** Pick up keys, put on shoes, then sit back down, so these stop predicting you leaving.
5. **Keep departures calm,** and give a long-lasting food toy if your dog will eat it when alone. Many anxious dogs won't, which is a good stress indicator.

## Things that don't help

- **Punishment.** It adds fear.
- **Getting another dog.** It helps some dogs but not most, because they're attached to you, not just company.
- **Crating a panicking dog.** Some dogs feel safer in a crate; others panic more and hurt themselves trying to escape.

## Talk to your vet

Separation anxiety often improves faster with **anti-anxiety medication** alongside training. A veterinarian, veterinary behaviorist or certified separation anxiety trainer can build a plan with you.
